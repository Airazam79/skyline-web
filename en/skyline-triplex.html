The apartment

The Triplex is spread over three floors. You come in on the middle floor, where the living room and kitchen are. A steel-and-timber staircase with a glass balustrade leads up to one bedroom and down to the other, each with a double bed. There is one bathroom with a shower.

The decor has a well-travelled feel: exposed brick, a wooden ceiling in the top-floor bedroom, a headboard made from an old door and decorated ceramic basins with gold-tone taps. The windows look onto an inner patio with bougainvillea.

It's the only one of the five Skyline apartments with a dishwasher.
