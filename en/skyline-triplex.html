The apartment

An original apartment with ethnic-inspired decor, spread over three floors right in the old town. It's an interior apartment that doesn't face the street. You come in on the middle floor, with the living room and kitchenette; the two bedrooms, each with a double bed, are upstairs and downstairs.

Its details set it apart: exposed brick walls, decorated ceramic basins, a wooden ceiling in the top-floor bedroom and a headboard made from an old door. Each bedroom has its own bathroom, and the downstairs one opens onto an interior patio. There's air conditioning in the bedrooms and living room, and it's the only Skyline with a dishwasher.
