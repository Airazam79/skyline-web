The official route, on foot

The official route runs through Plaza de la Constitución, Calle Larios, Martínez, Atarazanas, the Alameda Principal and Plaza de la Marina to the cathedral. Walking minutes from Calle Nueva 10:

  • 2 min

    Plaza de la Constitución

    The official route's main grandstand.

  • 2 min

    Calle Larios

    The stretch of the route right after the main grandstand.

  • 4 min

    Atarazanas Market

    The route runs along Calle Atarazanas, by the market, towards the Alameda Principal.

  • 4 min

    The cathedral

    End of the route, by the South Tower.

Practical tips

  • Timing

    Based on 2026 timings, most brotherhoods set out in the afternoon and return to their church after midnight.

  • Traffic

    In 2026 the Alameda Principal, Plaza de la Marina, Paseo del Parque, Calle Córdoba and Calle Boquete del Muelle were closed to traffic during processions, and the City Council recommended public transport.

  • Arriving without a car

    Calle Nueva is pedestrian. Centro-Alameda train station, with the airport line, is 11 minutes away on foot.

  • Public holidays

    Maundy Thursday, 25 March, and Good Friday, 26 March 2027, are public holidays in Andalusia.

Questions about Holy Week on Calle Nueva

When is Málaga Holy Week in 2027?

From Palm Sunday, 21 March, to Easter Sunday, 28 March 2027. Maundy Thursday and Good Friday, 25 and 26 March, are public holidays in Andalusia.

Do processions pass along Calle Nueva?

Yes. In 2026 the official routes of seven brotherhoods went along Calle Nueva itself on their way to or from the official route: three on Palm Sunday, one on Holy Monday, one on Holy Tuesday and two on Good Friday.

Can you see them from the apartment?

Skyline 2, Skyline 3 and Skyline Jacuzzi have balconies over Calle Nueva, and from them you can watch the processions that pass along the street.

How far is the official route?

Plaza de la Constitución, with the main grandstand, and Calle Larios are a 2-minute walk away. The cathedral, where the route ends, is 4 minutes away.

How do I get there if roads are closed?

Calle Nueva is pedestrian, so you can't drive to the door. Centro-Alameda train station is 11 minutes away on foot and the Camas public car park 4.

Where is the best price?

On the official website. The direct rate is always the lowest, because it carries no intermediary commissions.
