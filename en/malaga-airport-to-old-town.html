From Málaga airport to Calle Nueva

Train, bus and taxi to the old town

From Málaga airport, the quickest option by public transport is the C1 local train: 12 minutes to Málaga-Centro Alameda, the last stop, then 11 minutes on foot to Calle Nueva 10. The Line A Airport Express bus costs €4 and stops at Alameda Principal - Sur, 6 minutes away on foot. Calle Nueva is pedestrianised, so taxis and cars can't reach the door.

Before you leave the airport

  • Pay by card on the train

    You can pay for a single ticket at the gates with a contactless card or your phone. Tap again when you exit, or you'll be charged the maximum fare.

  • Arriving late at night

    There are no trains between 00:54 and 06:44, and no buses between the last departure (01:10 or 01:15) and 07:00. At those times, take a taxi.

  • Taxi

    There's no fixed fare to the centre: you pay the meter. Add €0.58 per suitcase in the boot and, from midnight to 6 am, €2.23 on the night and holiday fares.

  • Check-in

    Check-in is from 16:00 with an electronic lock. Earlier access isn't possible.

Questions about getting here

What's the fastest way from the airport to Calle Nueva by public transport?

The C1 local train: 12 minutes to Málaga-Centro Alameda, then 11 minutes on foot to Calle Nueva 10.

How much is the train?

€1.80 for a single ticket: that's Renfe's fare for 1 or 2 zones, and the trip runs from zone 1 (Airport) to zone 0 (Málaga-Centro Alameda). You can pay with a contactless card at the gates.

How much is the bus?

€4, in cash or by card on board, or with a ticket in the EMT Málaga app. Luggage travels free.

How much is a taxi?

There's no fixed fare: you pay the meter. On weekdays from 6 am to 10 pm the minimum fare from the airport is €18.24 (City Council fares, March 2026).

What if I land in the middle of the night?

There are no trains between 00:54 and 06:44, and no buses between the last departure (01:10 or 01:15) and 07:00. At those times you'll need a taxi.

Can a taxi drop me at the door?

No. Calle Nueva is pedestrianised and cars can't reach the door.
