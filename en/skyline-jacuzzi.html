The apartment

A modern, elegant studio in a fully restored historic building on Calle Nueva, one of Málaga's most storied streets. Next to the bed is a private whirlpool bath.

It's an open-plan space with a double bed, a fully equipped kitchenette and a bathroom with a shower. It also has an Andalusian patio with outdoor furniture to enjoy Málaga's climate in the open air.

Its balconies face Calle Nueva, a key street during Holy Week: processions pass along it and can be watched from the apartment itself.
