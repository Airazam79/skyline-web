Skyline Triplex, three floors at Calle Nueva 10

Before you book

  • Stairs. The three floors are connected by stairs.
  • Bedrooms without doors. They open onto the staircase; the bathrooms do have doors. If two couples want more privacy, look at the two-studio option.
  • Interior. The Triplex doesn't face the street.
Payment
30% is paid when you book. Four days before arrival you'll get an email to complete full payment of the booking.
Check-in
With an electronic lock, from 16:00. Earlier access isn't possible. Check-out by 12:00.
Linen and cleaning
There are always clean sheets and one set of towels per guest; extra towels are available for an additional charge. Stays of under 7 nights don't include cleaning or towel and sheet changes. From 7 nights, sheets and towels are changed.
Deposit
€150 for any incidents caused by guests during the stay.
Cancellation
Flexible rates can be cancelled free of charge up to 4 days before arrival; after that, part of the booking is charged. Non-refundable rates allow no date changes, and cancelling them costs 100% of the stay. For stays of more than a month, the cancellation period is one month. Questions about the apartment for four

How many people can stay in Skyline Triplex?

Four, in two bedrooms with double beds.

How many bathrooms does it have?

Two: each bedroom has its own bathroom.

Do the bedrooms have doors?

No. They open onto the staircase, on different floors. The bathrooms do have doors.

Is there a dishwasher?

Yes. The Triplex is the only Skyline with a dishwasher.

Is there another option for four in the building?

Yes: booking two studios for two, such as Skyline 2 and Skyline 3. Each couple gets its own studio, with its own door, bathroom and kitchenette.

Can I park nearby?

Calle Nueva is pedestrian, so you can't drive to the door. The Camas public car park is 4 minutes away on foot.
