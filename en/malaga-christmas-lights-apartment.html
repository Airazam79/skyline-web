Christmas in Málaga, two minutes from the Larios lights

See the lights and walk back home

The Calle Larios Christmas lights are a 2-minute walk from Skyline at Calle Nueva 10. In 2025 the switch-on ceremony began on Friday 28 November at 18:30, and the light and music show ran three times a day until 4 January, at 18:30, 20:30 and 22:00, except on 24 and 31 December. The 2026 dates have not yet been confirmed by the City Council.

Christmas in the old town, on foot

Walking minutes from Calle Nueva 10, with what was on over Christmas 2025-26:

  • 2 min

    Calle Larios

    The «Natividad de luz» light and music show, at 18:30, 20:30 and 22:00 until 4 January, except 24 and 31 December.

  • 2 min

    Plaza de la Constitución

    Christmas music from 20 to 29 December (except the 24th) and, on New Year's Eve, the chimes with free grapes and party bags while stocks last.

  • 4 min

    The cathedral

    The «El pescador de sueños» projection show on the south tower, at 19:00, 20:30 and 22:00, until 4 January (except 24 and 31 December).

  • 13 min

    Muelle Uno

    The harbour promenade, for a walk between shows.

Dates to note

  • Switch-on

    In 2025 the ceremony began on Friday 28 November at 18:30. For 2026, local broadcaster 101TV reports it as expected on Friday 27 November, not yet officially confirmed.

  • Municipal Nativity scene

    In 2025-26 it was free to visit from its opening on the evening of 5 December until 6 January in City Hall's Patio de Banderas, with over 400 figures.

  • Three Kings parade

    On 5 January 2026 it went along the Alameda Principal and Calle Puerta del Mar, a short walk from Calle Nueva.

  • Public holidays

    In Andalusia: Monday 7 (moved from the 6th), Tuesday 8 and Friday 25 December 2026, and 1 and 6 January 2027.

Practical tips

  • Crowds on Larios

    In 2025 the local police planned over 1,300 extra shifts across the Christmas period, including crowd control on and around Calle Larios.

  • Traffic

    In 2025, during the Larios shows, the Alameda Principal or Paseo del Parque could be briefly closed to private traffic. Calle Nueva is pedestrian.

  • Getting here

    Centro-Alameda station, with trains to the airport, is 11 minutes away on foot, and the Camas public car park 4.

  • Climate

    All the apartments have heating and air conditioning.

Questions about Christmas on Calle Nueva

When are the Larios lights switched on in 2026?

The City Council has not confirmed it yet. Local broadcaster 101TV reports it as expected on Friday 27 November 2026. In 2025 the switch-on ceremony began on Friday 28 November at 18:30.

How far are the Larios lights?

Calle Larios is a 2-minute walk from Calle Nueva 10, and Plaza de la Constitución is 2 minutes away too.

What time is the light and music show?

Over Christmas 2025-26 there were three shows a day until 4 January, at 18:30, 20:30 and 22:00, except on 24 and 31 December. The 2026 times have not been published yet.

Can the rooftop hot tub be used in winter?

Yes. Skyline Rooftop Jacuzzi's hot tub is heated and can be used all year round, with no set hours.

Which days are public holidays?

In Andalusia: 7, 8 and 25 December 2026, and 1 and 6 January 2027.

Where is the best price?

On the official website. The direct rate is always the lowest, because it carries no intermediary commissions.
