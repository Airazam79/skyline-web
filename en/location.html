Calle Nueva 10, in Málaga's old town

Calle Nueva was laid out after the Christian conquest of Málaga to link the port with the main square, today Plaza de la Constitución. Until Calle Larios was opened, it was the city's main and busiest shopping street, home to tailors, silversmiths, silk merchants and linen sellers from day one.

It is still pedestrian and full of shops, running parallel to Larios. Skyline's building, at number 10, is more than a century old and has been restored keeping its balconies and exposed brick; Skyline 2 and Skyline 3 also keep the original hydraulic tiles. From the front door you can walk to almost everything visitors come to see in Málaga.

It's a lively area, by day and at night: you're right in the old town, a step from Calle Larios, Plaza de la Constitución, the cathedral and the Atarazanas Market. That buzz can be heard inside Skyline Jacuzzi and Skyline Rooftop Jacuzzi, where street sound reaches the studio. Skyline 2 and Skyline 3 are well insulated and the best choice if you're a light sleeper.

Getting here

  • From the airport

    The C1 local train takes about 12 minutes to Málaga Centro-Alameda station. From there it's 11 minutes on foot to the door. A taxi takes about 15 minutes without traffic.

  • From María Zambrano station

    Walk in along the Alameda Principal or take a short taxi ride.

  • By car

    Calle Nueva is pedestrian, so you can't drive to the door. The Camas public car park is 4 minutes away on foot.

The big dates, just around the corner

  • Holy Week

    Calle Nueva is a key street during Holy Week: processions pass along it, and the official route along Calle Larios and Plaza de la Constitución is 2 minutes away.

  • August Fair

    The centre's fair happens by day on Calle Larios and the surrounding streets.

  • Christmas

    The Calle Larios lights are two minutes away, and you walk back to the apartment afterwards.
