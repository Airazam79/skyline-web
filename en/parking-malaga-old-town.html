Where to park if you come by car

Public car parks within walking distance of Calle Nueva, and how to avoid fines

Calle Nueva is pedestrianised, so you can't drive to the door. The nearest public car park is SMASSA Camas, a 4-minute walk away and open 24 hours. To enter the old town you must drive straight to a public car park, which registers your number plate, and your car must meet Málaga's Low Emission Zone rules.

Driving in without a fine

  • Camera-controlled old town

    Only residents, authorised vehicles and drivers heading to a pay-by-the-hour public car park may enter. The car park registers your number plate.

  • Drive straight there

    If the car park is inside the old town, you can't stop or park anywhere else in the area on the way in or out. Calle Nueva is pedestrianised, so don't try to reach the door.

  • If it's full

    If you head to a car park inside the camera-controlled old town and it's full, ask the staff to log your number plate before you leave the area.

  • Low Emission Zone

    Cars with a 0, ECO or C label can enter freely. Cars without a label that aren't registered in Málaga have been barred since 30 November 2025, and cars with a B label from 30 November 2026. The fine is €200. Check your car's label on the DGT website.

Sources: Málaga Mobility Bylaw (Official Gazette no. 231, 29/11/2024), Special Protection Zones decree (Official Gazette no. 91, 15/05/2025) and City Council statement of 27/11/2025. Mobility Bylaw (Spanish) · Old town decree (Spanish) · LEZ statement (Spanish) · Low Emission Zone (Spanish)

Questions about driving

Can I drive to Calle Nueva?

No. Calle Nueva is pedestrianised and lies in the old town, where access is camera-controlled.

Where do I park?

The nearest public car park is SMASSA Camas, a 4-minute walk away and open 24 hours. Plaza de la Marina (6 minutes) and Tejón y Rodríguez (7 minutes) are also close.

Can I be fined for driving into the old town?

Yes, unless you go straight to a pay-by-the-hour public car park. The car park registers your plate; if it's full, ask the staff to log it before you leave. Entering without authorisation carries a €200 fine.

Can my car enter the Low Emission Zone?

With a 0, ECO or C label, yes. Without a label and not registered in Málaga, no, since 30 November 2025. With a B label and not registered in Málaga, no, from 30 November 2026.

How much does parking cost?

SMASSA charges by the minute. Camas, Tejón y Rodríguez and Avenida de Andalucía charge more on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays; Plaza de la Marina charges the same every day. The published official fare is from Official Gazette no. 241 of 2021, before VAT: check the price at the pay station or in the app.

Are there electric car chargers?

Muelle Uno lists charging points in its La Farola and Paseo de los Curas car parks, a 16-minute walk away. SMASSA doesn't publish current charging data.
