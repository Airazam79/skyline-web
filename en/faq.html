Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Call us on +34 951 196 922 or email info@living4malaga.com.

Where is Skyline Apartments?

At Calle Nueva 10, 29005 Málaga, a pedestrian street in the old town. Calle Larios is 2 minutes away on foot, Plaza de la Constitución 2 and the Atarazanas Market 4.

Is it a historic building?

Yes. According to the Spanish Cadastre (Catastro) it is more than a century old, and it stands on Calle Nueva, Málaga's main street until Calle Larios was opened. It has been restored keeping its balconies and exposed brick; Skyline 2 and Skyline 3 also keep the original patterned tiles. The other apartments don't have patterned tiles.

Which apartments have a jacuzzi?

Two. Skyline Jacuzzi has a whirlpool bath inside the studio, next to the bed. Skyline Rooftop Jacuzzi has an outdoor hot tub on a private rooftop.

How many people can stay?

The four studios (Skyline 2, Skyline 3, Skyline Jacuzzi and Skyline Rooftop Jacuzzi) sleep 2 in a double bed. Skyline Triplex sleeps 4: it has two bedrooms with double beds, each with its own bathroom. The bedrooms have no doors and open onto the staircase.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 16:00 and check-out is by 12:00.

How do I get in?

With an electronic lock. Once you complete online check-in we send you the access instructions, so there's no need to meet anyone for keys.

Is there a kitchen?

Yes. Every apartment has a kitchenette with fridge, microwave, coffee maker, kettle, toaster, cookware and a washing machine. The Triplex also has a dishwasher.

Is there air conditioning?

Yes, all apartments have air conditioning and heating. The Triplex has a unit in each bedroom and in the living room.

Are there stairs inside any apartment?

In two. In Skyline Rooftop Jacuzzi a very steep staircase leads up to the roof, and the Triplex's three floors are connected by stairs.

Can I park nearby?

Calle Nueva is pedestrian, so you can't drive to the door. The Camas public car park is 4 minutes away on foot.

How do I get here from the airport?

Take the C1 local train to Málaga Centro-Alameda (about 12 minutes), then walk for 11 minutes. A taxi takes about 15 minutes without traffic.

How do I pay?

30% is paid when you book, and four days before arrival you'll get an email to complete full payment of the booking.

Is cleaning or a towel change included?

There are always clean sheets and one set of towels per guest, and extra towels are available for an additional charge. Stays of up to 7 nights don't include cleaning or a change of towels and sheets. For 8 to 12 nights we leave spare bed linen and towels for you to change. From 13 nights we come once a week to change them.

Can I cancel?

Flexible rates can be cancelled free of charge up to 5 days before arrival; after that, the 30% paid when booking is not refunded. Non-refundable rates allow no date changes, and cancelling them costs 100% of the stay. For stays of more than a month, you need to cancel at least one month before arrival.

Is there a security deposit?

Yes, €150 for any incidents caused by guests during the stay.

What is the area like?

It's a lively area: Calle Nueva is a pedestrian street right in the old town, a step from Calle Larios, Plaza de la Constitución, the cathedral and the Atarazanas Market, with shops, terraces and people strolling by day and at night. In Skyline Jacuzzi and Skyline Rooftop Jacuzzi that buzz can be heard inside the studio. During Holy Week, the August Fair and Christmas the centre gets even busier.

Who manages the apartments?

Living4Malaga, a Málaga company specialising in holiday rentals. The building is listed in the Andalusian Tourism Register under number A/MA/01554.

Where do I book?

On living4malaga.com, the management company's official website. Each apartment page takes you straight to its booking page.

Where is the best price?

On the official website. The direct rate is always the lowest, because it carries no intermediary commissions; the difference varies by date.
