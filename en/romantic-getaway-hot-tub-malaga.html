A romantic getaway in Málaga with a private jacuzzi

Two studios for couples at Calle Nueva 10, right in the old town

Skyline has two studios for couples with a private jacuzzi: Skyline Jacuzzi, with a whirlpool bath next to the bed, and Skyline Rooftop Jacuzzi, with a heated outdoor hot tub on its own rooftop, usable all year round. Both are at Calle Nueva 10, a 2-minute walk from Calle Larios and 4 minutes from the cathedral.

Questions about the jacuzzi apartments

Which Skyline apartments have a jacuzzi or hot tub?

Two: Skyline Jacuzzi, with a whirlpool bath inside the studio next to the bed, and Skyline Rooftop Jacuzzi, with an outdoor hot tub on a private rooftop.

Is the jacuzzi private?

Yes. Skyline Jacuzzi's bath is inside the studio, and Skyline Rooftop Jacuzzi's rooftop is only for the guests staying in that apartment.

Can the rooftop hot tub be used in winter?

Yes. It's heated and can be used all year round, with no set hours.

Can you prepare something for a special occasion?

Yes, on request. If you're celebrating, tell us what you'd like when you book and we'll confirm what can be arranged.

How many people can stay?

Two. Both are studios with a double bed.

Where are they?

At Calle Nueva 10, a pedestrian street in Málaga's old town, a 2-minute walk from Calle Larios and Plaza de la Constitución.

Can you hear the street?

Yes. Calle Nueva is a busy pedestrian street, and in both studios street noise can be heard inside, at night too.

Can I park nearby?

Calle Nueva is pedestrian, so you can't drive to the door. The Camas public car park is 4 minutes away on foot.
