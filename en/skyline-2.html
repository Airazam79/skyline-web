The apartment

Skyline 2 is in a fully restored historic building on Calle Nueva, a few steps from the Carmen Thyssen Museum and the Atarazanas Market. What sets it apart is its yellow-and-blue decor, the balconies on the renovated façade and the original hydraulic tiles it keeps in the living area.

It's an open-plan studio with a double bed, a fully equipped kitchenette and a bathroom with a shower.

Who it suits

Couples and solo travellers who want the city at street level and a home with some history. It is well insulated from street noise.
