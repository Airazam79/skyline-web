The apartment

This is the top floor of the building. Inside is a sloping attic with timber beams, a skylight and arched windows, in stone grey and dusty pink. An internal staircase leads up to the rooftop, for this apartment's guests only, with an outdoor hot tub.

From the water you look out over the old town's roofs and the bell tower of one of its churches.

The studio has a double bed, a sofa, an equipped kitchenette and a bathroom with a shower.
