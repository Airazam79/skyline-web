The apartment

A studio full of character on Calle Nueva, a few steps from the Carmen Thyssen Museum and the Atarazanas Market: grey-and-pink decor, arched windows onto Calle Nueva and a sloping ceiling with timber beams and a skylight.

Its great asset is upstairs: a private rooftop terrace with its own hot tub, looking over the old-town roofs and the bell tower of one of its churches. It's reached from the apartment by a steep ladder.

Inside, an open-plan space with a double bed, a fully equipped kitchenette and a bathroom with a shower.
