The Málaga Fair, two minutes from your door

Apartments on Calle Nueva, by the city-centre fair

The Málaga Fair (Feria de Málaga) takes place in August and, under its by-law, includes the 19th, a local holiday in 2026 and 2027. Its daytime part, the city-centre fair, filled Calle Larios and Plaza de la Constitución in 2026 from 12:00 to 18:00, a 2-minute walk from Skyline at Calle Nueva 10. The 2027 dates have not been approved yet.

At night, to the fairground

  • Cortijo de Torres fairground

    The night fair is at the Cortijo de Torres fairground. In 2026 the marquees opened from 14:00 and closed between 02:00 and 06:00 (up to 07:00 on holidays and their eves, and always at 07:00 in the youth area).

  • Bus F

    In 2026 EMT bus line F left from the north side of the Alameda, between Calle Larios and Puerta del Mar, and ran to the fairground 24 hours a day for €2.

  • Taxi

    During the 2026 Fair the taxi rank at Puerta del Mar, next to Calle Nueva, stayed open.

  • Train

    Centro-Alameda station is 11 minutes away on foot. During the 2026 Fair Renfe added services on line C-1.

Sources: Málaga City Council's 2026 Fair proclamation and mobility plan; extra train services announced by Renfe. Times and routes for 2027 may change. Practical tips

  • No car needed

    The City Council asks visitors to use the car as little as possible during the Fair. Calle Nueva is pedestrian, so you can't drive to the door.

  • Road closures

    In 2026 Calle Especería, next to Calle Nueva, was closed to traffic from 12:00 to 18:00 every day of the Fair, with normal traffic back around 21:00.

  • Parking

    The Camas public car park is 4 minutes away on foot. During the 2026 Fair the council's city-centre car parks opened 24 hours.

  • Heat

    The Fair is in mid-August. All the apartments have air conditioning.

Questions about the Málaga Fair

When is the 2027 Málaga Fair?

The 2027 dates have not been approved yet. Under the local by-law it is held in August and includes the 19th, the anniversary of Málaga joining the Crown of Castile. In 2026 it ran from 15 to 22 August.

Where is the city-centre fair?

On Calle Larios, Plaza de la Constitución and other old-town squares; in 2026, from 12:00 to 18:00. Calle Larios is a 2-minute walk from Calle Nueva 10.

How do I get to the Cortijo de Torres fairground?

In 2026 EMT bus line F left from the north side of the Alameda, between Calle Larios and Puerta del Mar, and ran 24 hours a day for €2. The taxi rank at Puerta del Mar also stayed open.

Is there music at night in the old town?

In 2026 street music at the city-centre fair stopped at 18:00; after that it carried on only inside licensed venues. The night-time party is at the fairground.

Which apartment suits a group?

Skyline Triplex, for four: two double bedrooms, each with its own bathroom. It doesn't face the street.

Where is the best price?

On the official website. The direct rate is always the lowest, because it carries no intermediary commissions.
